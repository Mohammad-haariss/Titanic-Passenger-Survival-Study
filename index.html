Key Historical Discoveries

  • Gender Bias ("Women & Children First"): Female passengers were nearly 4x more likely to survive than male passengers due to strict lifeboat boarding protocols.
  • Socioeconomic Class Stratification: 1st Class passengers enjoyed priority access to the top boat deck and higher survival rates (63%) compared to 3rd Class passengers (24%) trapped below deck.
  • Family Dynamics: Passengers traveling in small family units (2 to 4 members) achieved higher survival rates than solo travelers or large families (>5 members) who struggled to remain together.
  • Port of Embarkation Effect: Passengers embarking at Cherbourg, France exhibited higher survival rates due to a significantly higher proportion of 1st class ticket holders.
